About

A general dealer that covers the floor.

FED General Dealer supplies shops and businesses with the lines they sell every day — from chicken, frozen, and paper to Da Vinci, Origin Organics, groceries, and workplace gear.

We operate from Sagewood Manor in Midrand. If you need a regular wholesale partner, or a rep who can take the order on site, that is the service.
